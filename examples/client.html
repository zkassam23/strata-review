Questions worth asking before you go firm

  1. Has the strata obtained updated quotes for the work in the minutes, and is a special levy resolution on the agenda for the next general meeting?
  2. Has the envelope remediation recommended by the engineer been completed, and is the documentation available?
  3. Is there a funding plan for the gap between the contingency balance and the depreciation report recommendation?
  4. What is the current water damage deductible, and has the strata considered a bylaw shifting responsibility to owners?
  5. Has the strata obtained legal advice on the rental restriction bylaw, and is an amendment planned?
  6. When is the next depreciation report update scheduled, and has it been budgeted?
